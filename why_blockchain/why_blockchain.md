**Why blockchain (Wüst–Gervais criteria):** state must be stored; multiple writers; writers mutually distrust; no desired trusted third party; public verifiability valuable — Enigma meets all five (balances/listings/escrow/reputation are shared state among distrusting buyers/sellers, no escrow company, auditability is the point). **Suitability metrics:** disintermediation, trustlessness, immutability/auditability, transparency, availability, interoperability (standard ERC-20 + EIP-2612). **Usable use case:** escrow+reputation generalizes to resale/freelance/deposits; deployed on Sepolia. **Stands out** by being built only on audited standards with explicit, enforced, reproducibly-tested security.

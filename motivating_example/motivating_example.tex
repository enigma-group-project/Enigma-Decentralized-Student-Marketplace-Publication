Consider two students, Alice and Bob, who have never met. Alice lists a used textbook; Bob wants to buy it with campus credits. On a conventional platform a trusted intermediary custodies Bob's payment, decides whether Alice delivered, and records reputation---a single point of control, fees, and failure. Without such an intermediary, Bob risks paying for an item that never arrives, while Alice risks shipping before payment is assured. Enigma removes the intermediary: Bob's ENGC is locked in a smart-contract \emph{escrow} the instant he purchases; Alice can verify the funds are committed; and the contract releases them to Alice only when Bob confirms delivery, or refunds Bob after a timeout if she never delivers. Every step---issuance, listing, escrow, and the resulting rating---is recorded on a public ledger that neither party, nor any platform operator, can silently alter.

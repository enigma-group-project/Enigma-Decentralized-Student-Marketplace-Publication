Two strangers, Alice (seller) and Bob (buyer), trade a textbook. A conventional platform is a trusted intermediary that custodies payment, judges delivery, and records reputation — a single point of fees and failure. Enigma locks Bob's ENGC in a smart-contract escrow at purchase, releases to Alice only on confirmed delivery (or refunds Bob after a timeout), and records every step on a public, tamper-evident ledger — no intermediary.

\textbf{Owner: Member~4 (Omair Naeem).} The \texttt{Reputation} contract maintains an on-chain rating ledger intentionally decoupled from escrow, following the single-responsibility principle.

\textbf{Architecture.} Two functions are exposed: \texttt{rateUser(listingId, rating)} allows the verified buyer of a completed trade to submit a 1--5 star rating for the seller exactly once per listing; \texttt{getAverageRating(user)} returns the raw accumulator pair \texttt{(total,~count)}, leaving division to the client to avoid integer truncation and save gas. State is stored in three mappings: \texttt{ratingTotal[seller]}, \texttt{ratingCount[seller]}, and \texttt{listingRated[listingId]}. Authentication is performed by reading \texttt{Marketplace.getListing(id)} and verifying \texttt{msg.sender == listing.buyer} and \texttt{listing.status == Status.Sold}.

\textbf{Threat Model.} Four attack vectors are addressed. (1)~\emph{Rating manipulation}: the \texttt{NotBuyer} guard checks \texttt{msg.sender} against \texttt{listing.buyer}, recorded atomically during \texttt{purchaseItem}. (2)~\emph{Double rating}: the \texttt{listingRated} flag and \texttt{AlreadyRated} revert prevent score inflation via the CEI pattern. (3)~\emph{Reentrancy}: the \texttt{nonReentrant} modifier from OpenZeppelin's \texttt{ReentrancyGuard} guards the external \texttt{getListing} call. (4)~\emph{Unsold listing rating}: the \texttt{NotSold} guard ensures ratings only attach to completed trades.

\textbf{Trade-off Evaluation.} Storing \texttt{(total,~count)} and computing averages client-side preserves precision and saves ${\sim}5{,}000$ gas over on-chain division. Placing \texttt{Reputation} in its own contract enables independent upgrades and cleaner CODEOWNERS boundaries. Cross-contract authentication via \texttt{getListing} is trustless, requiring no oracle or relayer---consistent with the no-backend constraint. Measured gas cost: \textbf{120,280~gas} (\texttt{rateUser}, Anvil \texttt{forge test}); estimated Sepolia confirmation latency ${\sim}12$~s. The higher-than-estimated cost (vs.\ ${\sim}65{,}000$) reflects the cross-contract \texttt{getListing} call and loading string fields into memory.

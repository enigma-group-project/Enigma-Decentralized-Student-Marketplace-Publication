Decentralized marketplaces (e.g., OpenBazaar) and Ethereum smart contracts enable escrow/tokens without a central operator. ENGC follows EIP-20 + EIP-2612; security is inherited from audited OpenZeppelin libraries rather than hand-rolled. Atzei et al. catalogue Ethereum attack classes (reentrancy, ordering) that motivate our CEI + guards; Slither + Foundry provide static analysis and unit/fuzz/invariant testing. Our contribution: a teaching-oriented, fully on-chain token+escrow+reputation marketplace built from independently owned vertical slices with reproducible tests.

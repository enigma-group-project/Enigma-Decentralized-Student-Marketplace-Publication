We evaluate Enigma along five defender-relevant axes: \emph{functional correctness} (unit-test pass rate over behaviors and revert conditions); \emph{security robustness} (Slither~\cite{feist2019slither} finding count and the holding of fuzz/invariant properties such as monotonic listing identifiers and no self-purchase); \emph{gas cost} per state-changing operation; \emph{confirmation latency} (time-to-first-confirmation); and \emph{modularity} (independent testability of each slice)---together capturing the correctness, safety, and cost dimensions that determine deployability.

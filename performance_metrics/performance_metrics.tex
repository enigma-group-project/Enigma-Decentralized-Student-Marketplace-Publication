We evaluate Enigma along five defender-relevant axes: (1) \emph{functional correctness}---the pass rate of unit tests over the contract behaviors and revert conditions; (2) \emph{security robustness}---the count of Slither~\cite{feist2019slither} findings and the holding of fuzz/invariant properties (e.g., monotonically increasing listing identifiers and the impossibility of self-purchase); (3) \emph{gas cost} per state-changing operation; (4) \emph{confirmation latency}, measured as time-to-first-confirmation; and (5) \emph{modularity}---whether each slice is independently testable. Together these capture correctness, safety, and cost, the dimensions that determine whether the design is deployable.
